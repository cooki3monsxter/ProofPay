# Real-user testing kit (for the Potential Impact score)

Judges score impact on "a credible, specific case for solving a real problem for a real audience". Five to ten
real people trying ProofPay is the strongest evidence you can add. **Only report what people actually said.**

## Who to ask

- 3–5 freelancers (ideally ones who bill clients abroad): classmates who freelance, NEXUS MUJ members, people
  from freelancer communities.
- 2–3 people who have hired a freelancer (small business owners, startup founders, seniors running projects).

## Session (15 minutes, video call or in person)

1. One sentence of context: "It's a way to pay for freelance work where the money is held by PayPal and released
   when the delivery is checked."
2. Ask before showing anything:
   - "Have you ever not been paid, or paid late, for freelance work? What happened?" (freelancers)
   - "Have you ever paid for work you didn't get, or worried you would?" (clients)
3. Let them use the hosted demo (sandbox) without help:
   - Client: create an escrow from the sample scope and fund milestone 1.
   - Freelancer: open the freelancer link and submit proof.
   - Freelancer: copy the track record link and open it.
4. Ask:
   - "Would you use this for your next job? Why or why not?"
   - "What would stop you?"
   - "Was anything confusing?"
   - Freelancers: "Would you share the track record link with a new client?"

## What to record (a simple sheet)

| # | Role | Has had payment trouble? | Would use it? (yes / maybe / no) | Main reason | Confusing part | Quote OK to publish? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Consent line (say or send it)

"Can I quote what you said in my hackathon submission, with your first name (or anonymously)?"

## How to use the results

- In `docs/DEVPOST.md`, replace the `IMPACT EVIDENCE` comment with one or two sentences, e.g.
  "We asked 8 people to try it; 6 said they'd use it for their next client. The most common reason: …"
- Add one short quote, with permission.
- Fix the most common confusing part before recording the video.
- If people say no, that's useful too — one line about what you changed because of it reads as credible.
